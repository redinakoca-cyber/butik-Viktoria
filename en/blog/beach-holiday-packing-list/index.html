The holiday suitcase: what to pack for the coast in 2026

Victoria Boutique · 22 July 2026 · Durrës

For a week at the coast you need fewer clothes than you think — but the right ones: two light dresses that go from beach to dinner, natural fabrics that won't betray you in the heat, and one elegant outfit for the special evening. Here's the full list, tested by a boutique that lives by the sea.

We're from Durrës — we know the coast well. Every summer we watch the same scene: a full suitcase and yet "nothing to wear". The problem isn't quantity, it's selection. Here's how to get it right.

What must be in the suitcase?

  • Two light multi-purpose dresses. Over a swimsuit by day, with sandals and earrings by night. Linen or cotton — never heavy synthetics.
  • A jumpsuit or wide linen skirt. The quick change that looks intentional even when it isn't.
  • Two or three neutral tops. That combine with everything else in the suitcase — white, beige, blue.
  • One "big evening" outfit. The anniversary dinner, the surprise party, the wedding added to the programme — there's always one. One elegant dress saves you.
  • A light scarf or jacket. The evening breeze by the sea doesn't care how hot it was at lunch.

Which fabrics survive the heat?

The rule is simple: if the fabric doesn't breathe, it stays home. Linen is the king of July — it absorbs, dries fast, and its creases are part of the charm. Cotton is the safe classic. Quality viscose falls beautifully and stays cool. This is also why Greek clothing works so well on our coast — it's made for exactly this climate, as we explain in the article on Greek quality.

The classic suitcase mistakes

  1. "I might wear it." No, you won't. Every piece must combine with at least two others — otherwise it stays in the suitcase.
  2. Heels only. Coastal cobblestones and sand demand elegant flat sandals; save the heels for one evening.
  3. Last-minute shopping at the resort. Tourist prices, questionable quality. Better to order a week ahead — here's how it works.

Beach to dinner: the 5-minute formula

The linen dress + strappy sandals + gold earrings + lipstick. That's it. There is no other secret — just good foundational pieces. And a little sunshine, which the Albanian coast gives away for free.
