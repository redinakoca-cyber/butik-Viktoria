Buying Guide

How to buy original Greek fashion online in Albania

Victoria Boutique · 22 July 2026 · Durrës

You can order original Greek clothing anywhere in Albania from Victoria Boutique in Durrës: choose a piece in the online shop, message us on WhatsApp, and pay only when the parcel reaches your door. No bank card, no prepayment, no risk.

Since 2012 we have been bringing hand-picked clothing from Greece — dresses, blouses, skirts and special-occasion wear from Greek brands trusted for their cut and quality. This guide walks you through exactly how ordering works.

How does an order work, step by step?

  1. Browse what's in store. The Shop page shows our current pieces with real photos and exact prices.
  2. Tap "Order on WhatsApp". Every item has its own button — the message opens pre-filled with the item's name; you just send it.
  3. We confirm the details together: size, colour and delivery address. Stuck between two sizes? Send us your measurements and we'll advise you.
  4. The courier brings your parcel. Delivery covers all of Albania and usually arrives within a few working days.
  5. Pay at the door. Only once the parcel is in your hands — that's it.

Why Greek fashion?

Greek fashion has an identity of its own: elegant Mediterranean cuts, light natural fabrics, and workmanship that outlasts a single season. Unlike mass production, the pieces we choose come from reputable Greek brands — the same ones you'd find in the boutiques of Athens and Thessaloniki. We've written more about this in "Why Greek fashion: quality that lasts".

Is buying online with cash on delivery safe?

Yes — and it's exactly why we work only with COD. You pay nothing up front: you see the parcel at your door and only then hand over the money. No bank card, no online account, no app required. How delivery works in detail is covered in our cash-on-delivery guide.

Can't find what you're looking for online?

The online shop shows part of what we carry in the boutique. If you're after something specific — a wedding-guest dress, workwear, a gift — message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you photos of what we have in your size. And if you're in Durrës, come see us: here's where to find us.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy without a bank card?

Yes. Payment is cash only, at the moment of delivery. We ask for no prepayment and no bank details.

How long does delivery take?

Courier deliveries usually arrive within a few working days anywhere in Albania, depending on your city.

How do I know the clothes are original?

We've worked only with trusted Greek brands since 2012, and every piece is hand-picked. Over a decade of returning customers is our best guarantee.

What if I'm not sure about my size?

Send us your measurements on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll advise you precisely. We've also prepared a full size guide.

Ready for your first order?

Message us on WhatsApp — we reply quickly between 9:00 and 20:00, Monday to Saturday. Or visit our boutique in Durrës.
