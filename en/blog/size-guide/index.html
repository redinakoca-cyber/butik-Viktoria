Size guide: order the right fit online, every time

Victoria Boutique · 22 July 2026 · Durrës

To choose the right size online you only need three body measurements — bust, waist and hips — taken with a tape measure in two minutes. Send them to us on WhatsApp along with the piece you like, and we compare them against the garment's real measurements. That way the parcel you open fits the first time.

The wrong size is the main reason people hesitate to buy clothes online — and the main cause of returns. We solve it with the oldest method in the world: we ask, we measure, we advise. Here's how to do your part in two minutes.

How to take your measurements at home

All you need is a tailor's tape measure. Stand straight, in front of a mirror, wearing thin clothing or underwear:

  • Bust: measure around the fullest part, with the tape parallel to the floor. Don't pull tight — one finger should fit underneath.
  • Waist: the narrowest part, usually just above the navel. Breathe normally — don't hold your breath!
  • Hips: around the fullest part, with your feet together.

Write all three down in centimetres. That's the hard part done.

Between two sizes — which one?

It depends on the cut and fabric:

  • Loose styles (summer dresses, wide Greek cuts): take the smaller size — the ease is part of the design.
  • Fitted styles (evening dresses, defined waists): the larger size often falls more elegantly and is easy to take in at a tailor.
  • Stretch fabrics forgive a size; pure linen and cotton do not.

Or simply ask us — we've seen every piece with our own eyes and often tried it on. We tell you honestly how it falls, not how it looks in a catalogue. That's part of how we work.

Why the right size = stress-free COD

With cash on delivery you risk nothing financially. But the courier can't fix a wrong size at your door. Two minutes with a tape measure is the difference between "worn on the first evening" and "needs exchanging". Do it once, save the numbers in your phone, and every future order — with us or anywhere — becomes a thirty-second job.

Frequently asked questions

I don't have a tape measure — what now?

Use a cord or ribbon and measure it against a ruler. Or drop by the boutique in Durrës — we'll measure you in five seconds.

Are Greek sizes different from European ones?

Greek brands typically use EU or S/M/L sizing, but cuts vary. That's why we always compare your measurements against the specific garment, not a generic table.

What if the size that arrives doesn't fit well?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you try it — we'll find a solution together. Our goal is that you wear it, not shelve it.
