A clothing boutique in Durrës: where to find original Greek fashion

Victoria Boutique · 22 July 2026 · Durrës

If you're looking for a clothing boutique in Durrës with original Greek fashion, Victoria Boutique has been here since 2012: dresses, blouses and elegant pieces hand-picked from trusted Greek brands, open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 – 20:00.

Durrës has more and more clothing shops — but few true boutiques, where every piece is chosen one by one and you're greeted by name. Whether you live here or you're visiting Albania's most storied coastal city for its beaches and its amphitheatre, here's what makes us different, what you'll find inside, and how to find us.

What separates a boutique from an ordinary shop?

In an ordinary shop you find quantity; in a boutique you find selection. We travel to Greece ourselves and hand-pick every piece — not whole collections by the crate, but the pieces that matter: the cut that falls beautifully, the fabric that lasts, the colour people ask for. It means you'll never see "warehouse stock" here — you see a curated wardrobe.

What will you find at Victoria Boutique?

  • Dresses — from easy summer dresses to elegant pieces for weddings and events. Got a wedding ahead? Read our guide for guests.
  • Blouses and skirts — light combinations for work and the city.
  • Seasonal pieces — light Mediterranean fabrics in summer, elegant layers in winter. See this season's trends.

All from original Greek brands — no copies, no doubtful quality. Why that matters is explained in "Why Greek fashion".

Come visit us

Monday – Saturday, 9:00 – 20:00 · Durrës. Or browse the pieces online and order from your sofa — pay when the parcel arrives.

Not in Durrës? No problem.

Half of our customers have never set foot in the store. They browse the online shop, order on WhatsApp, and the parcel reaches them by courier anywhere in Albania — Tirana, Vlora, Elbasan, Shkodra, anywhere. Payment happens only at delivery: here's how COD works.

Why have customers trusted us since 2012?

Fourteen years in the same city is not something a disappointing shop survives. Customers come back because they know what they'll find: quality that doesn't lose shape by the third wash, honest advice about size and style, and a word that is kept. When something doesn't suit you, we say so to your face — better one sale less than one customer lost.
