Our Story

Over a decade of curated style

Victoria Boutique is a women's fashion boutique in Durrës, Albania, selling original Greek clothing with cash on delivery (COD) across all of Albania since 2012.

Victoria Boutique was born in 2012 in the heart of Durrës with a single vision: to bring the best of Greek fashion directly to women who value quality, craftsmanship and style.

Every piece in our collection is carefully selected from trusted Greek designers and brands — clothing that celebrates femininity, made with care and designed to outlive passing trends.

From everyday elegance to special-occasion wear, we offer a curated wardrobe for every chapter of your story.

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know

Is it safe to order with cash on delivery?

Yes. You pay only when the parcel reaches your door, in cash — no card and no prepayment. All the risk stays with us.

Where do you deliver and how long does it take?

We deliver by courier across all of Albania, and orders usually arrive within a few working days.

Which languages can I message you in?

We serve you in Albanian, English and Italian — message us in whichever language you prefer.

How do I place an order?

Browse the items on the Shop page, tap the WhatsApp button, we confirm your size and address together, and you pay on delivery.
